## Entity and brand facts to repeat

Answer engines synthesize from consistent, repeated, sourceable facts. These are
the sentences we want a model to be able to say about us — stated identically
everywhere.

- We are:
- We are known for:
- Founded / based / scale:
- Named proof points:

**Rule:** never restate these facts in a new way. Consistency is the mechanism.

## Content standards

- [ ] Answers the query in the first 60 words
